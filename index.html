What I model

Weapons

  • Swords, greatswords, katanas and other melee weapons
  • Detail modelled where it shows: chips, inlay, wraps, bevels
  • Split into sensible parts (blade, fittings, grip) for easy re-texturing

UGC accessories

  • Hats, bags, necklaces and other avatar items
  • Kept under the 4,000 triangle limit with a single 1024 texture set
  • Recolours and variants of existing items

Texturing

  • Full PBR sets: color, normal, metalness and roughness
  • Realistic materials: forged steel, leather, silk, bronze, gold inlay
  • Ready to drop into a SurfaceAppearance

Renders & GFX

  • Studio product shots of your item for thumbnails and showcases
  • Scene GFX for games and groups

How I work

  • Triangle budget agreed up front: under 4k for UGC, up to 20k per mesh for game assets.
  • Clean topology, and wireframe screenshots available on request before you pay.
  • Exported as FBX at Roblox scale, so it imports at the right size with no fixing.
  • Close-up renders checked at every stage, and revisions until it looks right.
